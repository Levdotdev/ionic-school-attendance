Privacy policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

A student project, not an official university service

MinSU Attendance is administered as a student project. Its name and university seal do not mean that Mindoro State University operates, endorses, or has approved this deployment.

Who to contact

The MinSU Attendance student project administrator handles account and privacy requests at support.attendanceminsu@gmail.com. This is a project-owned Gmail address, not an official university email or university Data Protection Officer contact.

Information the project processes

  • Account information: first name, last name, email address, account role, approval status, and an optional profile picture.
  • Linked sign-in methods: provider identifiers and profile details supplied by Google or Facebook, such as name, email, and profile photo. Provider passwords are not provided to this project. Email/password sign-in is managed by Supabase Authentication.
  • School ID verification: the scanned school-ID barcode is submitted for verification and stored as a hash, not as a readable barcode number in the normal profile.
  • Attendance: classes, enrollment periods, schedules, present/absent records, submission times, teacher notes, and review decisions.
  • Photos and face verification: attendance selfies, a private face template used for enrollment and comparison, and verification results. Face analysis runs on the device; the template and verification information are stored in the project backend. Attendance photos are stored separately.
  • Location: coordinates and accuracy submitted when a student performs a self-check. On-site checks also use the school boundary. Manual teacher marking does not create a GPS reading.
  • Family connections and student work: parent invitations, parent approval decisions, task categories, tasks, due dates, links, attachments, and reminder preferences.
  • Device and service information: authentication sessions, notification device tokens when enabled, theme/navigation preferences, and technical information needed by hosting, authentication, storage, and delivery services.

Why this information is used

The implemented features use this information to authenticate accounts, protect role-based access, verify student check-ins, maintain attendance and schedules, support parent-approved family access, manage student tasks, deliver requested reminders, generate teacher reports, and investigate account or attendance problems.

Face matching can make mistakes. Teachers can review submitted attendance evidence and record attendance manually; students should contact their teacher or the project administrator about an incorrect result.

Who can access it

  • Students can access their own account, attendance, enrolled courses, and personal tasks.
  • Approved teachers can manage their own classes and review their students' attendance and photos. Class PDF/CSV exports are initiated by the teacher.
  • Parents must be registered and explicitly approve a student link before the app grants family access to that student's profile picture, attendance, photos, and class calendar. Students' personal tasks are not included in the parent calendar.
  • Online self-check location is shown only to the verified, approved linked parent in the application. It is not shown in student or teacher attendance views. On-site attendance coordinates are part of the authorized attendance evidence.
  • Project administrators manage approvals and verification access. Backend operators with privileged system access can administer the infrastructure; those privileges are separate from the app's ordinary role-based screens.

Parent-link approval controls family access. It is not a replacement for any notice, lawful basis, or consent required before a school processes identity, education, photo, biometric, or location information.

Services and device permissions

The project uses Supabase for authentication, database, private storage, and realtime updates; Vercel for website hosting; and Firebase/Google services for enabled mobile push notifications. Account and attendance emails use the project's configured email provider. Students receive attendance push alerts; verified, approved linked parents receive attendance push alerts and emails at their current verified account address. Administrators receive new teacher-request emails, and teachers receive approval-decision emails. These alerts do not include selfies, face templates, biometric verification data, or precise GPS coordinates; authorized details remain in the signed-in portal. Google and Facebook process their own sign-in flows when you choose those methods.

Camera and location permissions are requested for features that need them. You can change those permissions in your browser or device settings, although self-check may then be unavailable. Ask your teacher about manual attendance if you cannot complete a self-check. Session information and preferences may remain on the device until sign-out or local data is cleared.

Retention and removal

Records are kept while needed for an active account and the project's attendance or student-work purposes, and are reviewed when those purposes end or a verified removal request is received. This build does not provide a general automatic deletion schedule for attendance, photos, face templates, or location records.

The administrator must assess each request. If a documented school requirement or applicable legal obligation requires retaining a particular academic record, the administrator should explain what is retained, why, and the applicable retention criteria. There is no promise that all academic records can always be erased immediately. The project has not established an official university retention policy.

Teacher-exported files and data independently held by Google, Facebook, or other services are outside the app's direct control. Unlinking a sign-in method or uninstalling the app does not delete project records. See the data deletion instructions for requesting removal.

Questions, corrections, and privacy requests

Contact the project administrator to ask what information is held about you, request a correction or copy, object to processing, or request restriction or removal. Requests are assessed after identity and authority are verified; applicable exceptions may affect what can be removed. You may also contact the Philippine National Privacy Commission about privacy concerns.

This page describes this student project's current data flows. It is not a certification of legal compliance or authority to deploy the system as an institutional service. Before institutional use, the responsible school must confirm its controller details, lawful processing basis, retention rules, and any additional safeguards for children and biometric data.
