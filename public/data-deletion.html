Data deletion instructions

Last updated: October 26, 2026

A student project, not an official university service

MinSU Attendance is administered as a student project. Its name and university seal do not mean that Mindoro State University operates, endorses, or has approved this deployment.

Request removal from the project

You do not need to sign in to read these instructions or email a request.

  1. Email support.attendanceminsu@gmail.com, preferably from the email registered with your MinSU Attendance account. Use the subject MinSU Attendance data deletion request.
  2. Say whether you want the project account and associated information removed, or identify a specific category such as a profile photo, face template, attendance photo, submitted location, or personal task data. If you used Facebook or Google, say which sign-in method you used. Do not send the provider's password.
  3. The student project administrator will need to verify that the request is yours, or that you are authorized to act for the student. If you cannot access the registered email, explain that in the request so a safe verification method can be discussed.
  4. Ask for confirmation of the result, including any records that must be retained, the reason, and the retention criteria. Removal is a manually reviewed process, not an automatic action triggered by opening this page.
Compose a deletion request

This opens your email app. It does not send a message or delete anything automatically.

Do not send sensitive verification material in the first email.

Do not include passwords, login or recovery codes, barcode values, selfies, or identity-document images. Provide only the account email and a description of your request initially.

What the administrator needs to review

A verified request may involve the authentication account and linked-provider identifiers, profile and avatar, parent links, student ID hash, face template and verification information, stored photos and locations, personal tasks and attachments, device notification mappings, and attendance records.

Some academic records may need to be retained only where a documented school requirement or applicable legal obligation justifies that retention. The administrator should identify those records and explain the reason rather than promise that every record has been deleted. This project does not provide a fixed automatic deletion deadline.

Deleting data from this project does not delete your Facebook or Google account. It also cannot recall reports already downloaded or shared by an authorized teacher, or directly delete information independently held by another service.

Unlinking Facebook or Google is different

To remove a sign-in method while retaining the project account, open your profile menu, choose Security → Linked accounts, and remove the provider. Keep another working sign-in method available so you do not lose access. Removing a provider is not a request to delete your attendance or other project data.

You can also remove the project's Facebook connection in Facebook's Apps and Websites settings. After removing it, email the project administrator if you want project-held information deleted as well.

Read the privacy policy

The privacy policy explains the project's data categories, roles, location visibility, services, and retention criteria. This page provides deletion instructions only; it is not an automated Facebook data-deletion callback endpoint.
